1. About the app

myDynasty is a family tree and family memory app. It lets you build a visual family tree, attach photos and documents, record biographies, map places and burial sites, and import or export your data. The app does not require an account or registration.

Your family data — names, dates, photos, places, stories and documents — stays on your device, and we do not have a copy of it. Nothing from your tree leaves the device unless you export or share it yourself, or use a function that needs the internet: a map, the cemetery directory or an AI feature. When you use an AI feature, the content you choose to send goes to our server and from there to OpenAI, and only after you have agreed on that feature's consent screen. The Privacy Policy explains what each function sends and to whom.

AI features ("Capybara AI") are part of Premium on iPhone and iPad.

  • Only with your consent. Before an AI feature first sends your content, it shows a consent screen that lists what will be sent. No voice, photo or text is sent until you agree. What you send goes to our server and from there to OpenAI, as described in the Privacy Policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → "Capybara AI" → "Withdraw consents". Withdrawing consent does not cancel the subscription, and the extra room that Premium gives does not depend on agreeing to AI.

Photo restorations are counted separately: 4 per month, and unused ones carry over, up to 16. If the allowance or the restorations run out, the features concerned pause until the next monthly period; the rest of the app keeps working. Settings → "Capybara AI" shows what is left.

  • Restored photos are created by AI. A restored photo is a new image generated by AI. It may differ from how the person really looked, and it is not an original document. The original photo stays in the app.
  • Content you send. Send only content you have the right to share: photos you may use, and information about other people that you may lawfully share. Take particular care with details about living people, especially children.
